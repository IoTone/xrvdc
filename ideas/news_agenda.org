  SCHEDULED: <2026-06-16 Tue>

  :TAG:        STARTER
  :SLUG_EN:    moire-lens-varifocal-xr
  :SLUG_JA:    moire-lens-varifocal-xr-入門
  :SOURCE:     https://opg.optica.org/ao/abstract.cfm?uri=ao-47-21-3722
  :ANGLE:      User-requested + "do deep research" → ran the deep-research workflow
               (106 agents, 23 primary sources, 25 claims verified 0 killed) then a
               focused EXTRACTION agent over the vetted sources. Explainer, not a
               how-to: understand the mechanism + where it sits, honest "lab not
               shipping" thesis. PHYSICS (verified, Bernet & Ritsch-Marte, Applied
               Optics 47:3722, 2008, Innsbruck): two cascaded DOE/metasurface plates
               with complementary phase φ ∝ r²·φ and its inverse; neither is a lens
               alone; rotate one by θ → combined phase ∝ r²·θ = parabolic Fresnel
               lens; OPTICAL POWER LINEAR IN θ (1/f ∝ θ), passes through zero
               divergent↔convergent. Visible realization ~f=−36..+12mm over ±90°.
               FAMILY: Alvarez = translational analog (moiré = rotational); pancake
               = fixed focus; LC tunable (Meta Mirror Lake stack, FlexEnable) =
               electric, product-adjacent; liquid lens. WHY XR: VAC / gaze-driven
               varifocal / thin-flat AR. STATE OF ART (all LAB): PhotoniX 2025
               (Xiao HIT-Shenzhen / Mu Ku Chen CityU-HK / Geng Tsinghua) — 3
               cascaded TiO₂ metasurfaces, rotation-tuned focus+pupil-steer, 532nm,
               f=3.7–33.2mm, eyebox 4.2–5.8mm, 14.2% eff, AR demo, RGB = future;
               Iwami/Ogawa TUAT 2022 visible moiré metalens 64%/25%/8% @ 633/532/
               440nm. CONFLATION TRAP (load-bearing — the extraction agent caught 4
               errors in my brief): PhotoniX is NOT a "Lee/Kim Korean group";
               Optica-2022 "variable-focus lens" is LC Alvarez NOT moiré; Metalenz/
               Capasso = STATIC metasurfaces (Metalenz shipped product = ST depth
               SENSOR optic, not display/varifocal); Meta Mirror Lake = LC varifocal
               NOT moiré. Article explicitly de-conflates these. LIMITS: chromatic
               aberration (f∝1/λ, 64→8% red→blue) = central blocker; low eff/stray
               light/higher orders; Talbot-length spacing; 2µm misalignment distorts;
               no dynamic rotational actuator demoed (manual mounts); ~2mm apertures,
               e-beam fab not consumer scale. TAKEAWAY: NO moiré varifocal shipping;
               only shipped consumer metasurface = static sensor; "varifocal is
               coming" (LC/Alvarez, Meta ~2nd half decade) TRUE but "moiré shipping"
               FALSE. HEDGED/omitted: sciadv.abe4458 doublet numbers (403/search-
               only — kept qualitative, exact figures dropped). Cross-links: xreal,
               wwdc-2026, spatial-video (forward ref, publishes 06-13). Voice fix:
               removed "honest summary" → "accurate summary" (forbidden trope).

  Explainer on moiré rotational varifocal lenses for XR: two stacked diffractive/
  metasurface plates whose relative rotation tunes focal power linearly through
  zero (Bernet 2008; PhotoniX 2025 AR metalens). Where it sits vs Alvarez/pancake/
  LC; why XR cares (VAC); all lab, not shipping. Carefully de-conflates moiré from
  static metalenses (Metalenz/Capasso) and LC varifocal (Meta Mirror Lake). Held
  back by chromatic aberration, ~14% efficiency, sub-µm mechanical rotation.
  Deep-research workflow + extraction pass. Staged 2026-06-16; version 1.36 → 1.37.
