## XR VisionDevCamp 2026 — Event Overview

**Friday 26 June – Sunday 28 June, 2026** · Fukuoka, Japan.

A 2.5-day XR hackathon plus an AR Expo and pre-event training week, hosted across multiple sites in Fukuoka and culminating at **CIC Fukuoka / Fukuoka One Building**.

### Event Date

26 June (Fri) – 28 June (Sun), 2026

### Venue

- **Engineer Café — Hacker Space Fukuoka** (training, hacking floor)
- **CIC Fukuoka / Fukuoka One Building** (registration, keynote, presentations + judging)

### Schedule (Updated 6/23/2026)

- **Fri 17:00** — Doors open / registration check-in / device demos
- **Fri 19:00** — Keynote
- **Fri 19:00 – 21:30** — Mixer, team forming, in-person hacking
- **Fri 21:30** — Hackathon starter whistle blows. Hacking begins! Break till Saturday.
- **Sat 10:00** — Engineer Café doors open: onsite hacking
- **Sat 11:00** — Tech talks for development tracks (STYLY, WebXR, Godot, Unity, Lens Studio, Xcode)
- **Sat 12:30** — Lunch break (snacks / coffee outside)
- **Sat 16:00** — Team checkpoint
- **Sat 20:00** — Announcements: recap schedule, Sunday plan, team submissions
- **Sat 22:00** — Cleanup, close up for the evening. Keep hacking at home.
- **Sun 10:00** — Engineer Café opens
- **Sun 12:00** — Final project submissions due
- **Sun 13:00** — Cleanup, move to CIC
- **Sun 13:30** — CIC project presentations begin
- **Sun 15:00** — Break, judging
- **Sun 16:00** — CIC XR VisionDevCamp awards ceremony
- **Sun 17:00** — Cleanup
- **Sun 18:30** — "No-Host" dinner
- **Sun 20:00** — "No-Host" afterparty TBA

### Registration

Registration opens soon — sign up via the [contact form](/staging/contact/) for early notification.　　Registration on site is possible Friday and Saturday, but registration closes after Saturday of the event.
