STYLY is a platform for building and publishing Location-Based Entertainment (LBE) and immersive spatial experiences across VR, AR, and MR. Returning as a sponsor for 2026, STYLY provides XR devices and platform access to support participants throughout the event.

STYLY, Inc. is the provider of "STYLY," a spatial layer platform that seamlessly bridges the digital and physical worlds. Driven by its mission to "FREE INNER CREATIVITY," the company leverages XR technology to deliver a wide range of solutions — from WebAR to Location-Based Entertainment (LBE) — tailored for landowners and urban developers engaged in city planning. In collaboration with businesses and creators worldwide, STYLY aims to foster new cultures and industries, ultimately contributing to the evolution of humanity.
