We're pleased to welcome back **[STYLY](https://styly.cc)** as a sponsor of XR VisionDevCamp Fukuoka 2026. STYLY supported the 2025 edition, and returns this year providing **XR devices** and **access to the STYLY platform** to support participants throughout the event.

STYLY is a platform for building and publishing **Location-Based Entertainment (LBE)** and immersive spatial experiences across **VR, AR, and MR**. STYLY, Inc. is the provider of "STYLY," a spatial-layer platform that bridges the digital and physical worlds; driven by its mission to "FREE INNER CREATIVITY," the company delivers a range of solutions — from WebAR to LBE — for landowners and urban developers engaged in city planning.

## What participants get

- **XR devices** on hand during the event to build and test on.
- **Access to the STYLY platform** for authoring and publishing spatial experiences across VR, AR, and MR.
- **Resources to get started** — the platform, the developer documentation, and the open-source SDKs on GitHub.

## Make the most of STYLY

STYLY lets you author a scene once and play it across mobile AR, the web, Quest, and Vision Pro — with no custom C# required. For the full toolchain map — every authoring tool, which AR and VR targets each one reaches, and the constraints to design around before the hackathon — see our [STYLY SDK overview](/news/styly-sdks-ar-vr/).
