We're pleased to welcome **[Manabu Hubs](https://manabuhubs.com)** as a sponsor of XR VisionDevCamp Fukuoka 2026. Manabu Hubs supports founders entering the Fukuoka and APAC startup ecosystem, and their sponsorship brings concrete perks for participants.

## What participants get

- **Up to 6 months of Notion Business with Notion AI included** — a **$12,000 value** — for founders who apply through the Manabu Hubs referral link.
- **10% off** the Manabu Hubs incubator annual **APAC Founder plan**: market entry, perks, soft-landing, startup programs, a private network, and 1:1 mentoring.
- **Promotion across the Manabu Hubs network** — a 25k-subscriber newsletter, the Fukuoka Startup Collective Facebook and LinkedIn groups, and Discord + LINE channels.

The referral link and discount code are shared with registered participants.
